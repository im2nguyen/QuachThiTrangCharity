The 13th Quach Thi Trang scholarship ceremony will be held in 2026.

The Quach Thi Trang scholarship program has been held since 2014 to honor Quach Thi Trang, who died on August 25, 1963 in front of Ben Thanh Market while taking part in a demonstration against the religious discrimination policies of the government at that time. The program also helps grade-11 girls in difficult circumstances who are studious and the same age as Quach Thi Trang was when she died.

The 13th Quach Thi Trang scholarship program in 2026 was announced to 39 high schools in Ho Chi Minh City, and 26 schools have joined so far. All nominated students will receive scholarships: 62 scholarships at 3 million VND each and 38 special scholarships at 4 million VND each. The total funding of 338 million VND is provided by Quach Thi Trang Foundation.

The 2026 Quach Thi Trang scholarship ceremony will be held at 9 a.m. on Sunday, August 23, 2026, at the Quach Thi Trang monument in Bach Tung Diep Park, at the corner of Nam Ky Khoi Nghia and Ly Tu Trong streets in Ben Nghe Ward.
